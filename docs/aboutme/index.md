## What does it mean to defend an engineering decision?

To defend an engineering decision you need to justify why you made the choices that you did in order to solve a problem. These choices need to hold up under scrutiny to have successfully defended your design. Currently I believe I have some experience defending engineering decisions from my experience at the Wake Tech engineering club. During that club I had to defend design decisions for the payload housing that would be lifted to 90,000ft by a high altitude balloon. One of the major points of contention was how the top panel would come and and off. I argued for a simple approach using a 3d printed hole that screws could self thread into. It was lighter than using heat set inserts and although the threads wouldn't last very long if the screw would be constantly screwed and unscrewed the screws could be threaded once with the panel for a test fit and then only one more time on the day of the actual launch. The other option was a system where we would have used had a special points in the design of the lid for string to be tied around, but I argued this had a higher chance of going wrong if someone did a bad knot the entire top lid could come off whereas with screws the only thing you have to do is tighten them with a screwdriver and you know for sure its not coming off.
